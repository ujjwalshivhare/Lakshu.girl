Dil Se Ek Emotional Khat 💌

Suno... in saari dates ke baad bas itna kehna hai ki mujhe aapke saath har ek sapna poora karna hai. Ye dates aage bhi aise hi add-on hoti rahein, aur har naye din ke saath hamara rishta aur mazboot hota rahe.

Tum hi aakhiri ho, aur tum se hi sab kuch hai. Meri har subah, meri har koshish, aur meri har dua mein bas aap ho.

“Maine ek bahut pyaara sapna dekha hai—jab hamari nanhi si beti hogi, toh mujhe use apne haathon mein uthana hai. Jab main use dheere-dheere pehli baar uthaunga, kyunki wo bohot sensitive aur chhota sa baby rahegi, us pal ko main dil mein sambhalna chahta hoon. Aur aapke saath hi apna poora budhapa bitana chahta hoon—haath mein haath liye, har khushi aur har gham mein saath.”

Galti hui hai, dil dukhaya hai, par pyaar mein kabhi kami nahi thi aur kabhi hogi bhi nahi. Please mujhe sambhal lo.
